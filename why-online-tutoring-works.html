Why Online Instruction: Tom Quinn's perspective

Algebra • Geometry • Middle School Mathematics • Computer Science

One-on-one tutoring sessions conducted live via Zoom or In-Person with an Experienced Certified Educator.

Effective, Flexible, and Personalized

1. Online tutoring allows students to receive focused one-on-one instruction.

2. No travel time.

3. No scheduling complications.

Individual Attention

1. Students work directly with the tutor in a structured setting.

2. Questions can be answered immediately.

3. Lessons can move at the student’s pace.

Live Problem Solving

  • Using Zoom: students can;
  • share screens
  • review assignments
  • work through problems step by step in real time.

    • Convenience

    Families can schedule tutoring sessions from home, making it easier to fit academic support into busy weekly routines.

    Consistent Support
